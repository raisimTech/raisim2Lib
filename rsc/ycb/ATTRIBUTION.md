Each `<object>/google_16k` folder keeps the project's 16k-polygon scan
(`textured.obj`, `textured.mtl`, `texture_map.png`, `textured.dae`,
`nontextured.ply`, `nontextured.stl`, `kinbody.xml`).

Changes made: the `<object>.urdf` files, the convex collision meshes
(`textured_vhacd.obj`) and the CoACD decompositions (`raisim_coacd_*.obj`) are
added for simulation.
