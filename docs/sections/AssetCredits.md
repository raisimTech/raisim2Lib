# Asset Credits

The images and videos on this site are RaiSim simulations rendered with rayrai.
Most of the models, textures and skies in them are CC0, which asks for no
credit, or RaiSim's own. The assets below are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(CC BY 4.0) or ask for credit, and are credited here as their licenses require.
The full notices, including every change made to the originals, ship with the
assets in the `rsc` folder of
[raisim2Lib](https://github.com/raisimTech/raisim2Lib).
