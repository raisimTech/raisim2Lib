# Changelog: v2.10

## v2.10.3 (2026-10-25)

Rebuild applications after updating (`raisim::World` ABI change).

**RaiSim**

- Build: the Linux x86-64 libraries are built for the x86-64-v3 instruction set (AVX2, FMA, BMI1 and BMI2, F16C, LZCNT, MOVBE) instead of the CPU of the machine that built them (`-march=native`), so which CPUs a release runs on no longer depends on that machine. See [Introduction](../Introduction.md).
- **Behaviour change:** articulated systems integrate with `IntegrationScheme::SEMI_IMPLICIT` by default (it was `TRAPEZOID`): the positions move with the step's new velocity, the one the contact solver leaves, so an impact that stops a body stops it at the surface instead of half a step's travel in. Against analytic or converged solutions it was as accurate or more accurate wherever the forces depend on the state. At a 1 ms step, an undamped pendulum was off by 2 mrad after 10 s (126 mrad with `TRAPEZOID`, whose swing grew 9% in 20 s), a box tumbling about its middle axis by 0.007 mrad after 4 s (471 mrad), a falling 10-link chain by 62 mrad after 1 s (209 mrad), and a PD-controlled arm tracking a sine by 1.0 mrad (3.3 mrad). `TRAPEZOID` stays exact under a constant force: a projectile is 4.9 mm low after 1 s of flight with `SEMI_IMPLICIT` (24.5 mm at a 5 ms step). A PD-controlled system that has been in contact now keeps `SEMI_IMPLICIT`; it was integrated with `TRAPEZOID` whatever its scheme. `SEMI_IMPLICIT` also takes the place `TRAPEZOID` took for `EULER` and `RUNGE_KUTTA_4` there, and for `RUNGE_KUTTA_4` on systems with loop or mimic constraints. Simulations, and policies trained in them, move differently. A system on the default scheme gets its previous motion back with `setIntegrationScheme(IntegrationScheme::TRAPEZOID)`; a PD-controlled one that requested `SEMI_IMPLICIT`, `EULER` or `RUNGE_KUTTA_4` moved with `TRAPEZOID` after its first contact, which no setting reproduces now (see [Dynamics and Control](../articulated_system/DynamicsAndControl.md)). Benchmarks: the articulated ones changed by -11% to +2.5%, except `atlas_ground` (Atlas falling into a heap in torque mode), which went from 4.2 s to 22.5 s. Atlas lands in a different heap, with its legs crossed, where the contact solver runs 131 iterations a step instead of 9; either scheme continues that heap at the same cost, and over 25 starting poses the mean was 71 iterations with `SEMI_IMPLICIT` against 56 with `TRAPEZOID`.
- Fix: swept CCD left a body that a speculative contact had stopped resting just above the surface, out of reach of the discrete detection, and a sweep from where it rested found nothing. Pushed down on a later step (a foot under its leg), it went into the ground by the step's travel: 9 mm for a standing ANYmal's feet at a 5 ms step. A body within `sweptCcdSpeculativeMargin` of the terrain keeps speculative contacts, at a box's corners and on a cylinder's rims as touching contacts would be. With swept CCD on, a standing ANYmal costs 1.3% more a step, and 128 boxes and cylinders resting on the ground 29% more: the solver works three iterations a step on them instead of one.
- Fix: with sleeping enabled (the default), a moving kinematic body passed through sleeping bodies without waking them. A box asleep on a kinematic platform floated in place when the platform moved down and was passed through when it moved up, and a kinematic pusher swept through a sleeping box. A moving kinematic body (non-zero velocity, or a pose set since the last step) now wakes the sleeping islands its collision bodies reach between the last step and the end of this one, including what slept on a platform that `setPosition()` moved away, and keeps the islands it touches awake. See [World](../WorldSystem.md).
- Fix: a sleeping body switched to `BodyType::KINEMATIC` stayed asleep and ignored the velocity it was given; it now stops sleeping and moves.
- Fix: restoring a World checkpoint shrank an articulated system's contact Jacobians to the sizes they had at the capture. A later contact on a body deeper in the tree than the captured one (a foot after a contact on the base, for example) then wrote past them and corrupted the heap. Assigning a `SparseJacobian` now keeps its storage, growing it to the source's capacity if needed; `SparseJacobian::size` starts at zero.
- Fix: contacts between a convex mesh and a triangle mesh, between two triangle meshes, and between two convex meshes were placed at support points of the bodies along the contact normal. Where faces touch flat, those are arbitrary vertices of the faces, up to a metre outside both bodies. A default `World::addMesh()` box sank into a static triangle-mesh floor, a triangle-mesh box into a triangle-mesh plate, and convex meshes stacked or not depending on which was created first. Contacts are now placed at the EPA witness points, inside the overlap of the touching faces, and two convex meshes touching face to face get up to four contacts spread over the corners of their overlap instead of one, so they no longer tip about a single point (a disc, crossed bars and stacked boxes rest). `contact::EPAResult` has new `witnessA` and `witnessB` members, taken from the face of the polytope that holds its point nearest the origin. Piles of convex meshes carry somewhat more contacts: `mesh_collider_plane` takes 3% longer, and in `mesh_collider_speed` the dropped hulls now come to rest leaning on each other instead of being knocked apart, which makes its ground scene 2.2 times as long. `g1_hand_self_collision` keeps its mean step time, its 99th percentile goes from 100 to 115 µs. See [Collision Detection and Colliders](../CollisionDetection.md).
- Fix: two convex meshes touching in a symmetric pose (equal boxes stacked aligned, bars crossed at a right angle) could get a horizontal contact normal with no depth: EPA oriented the face of its starting polytope that passes through the origin by the sign of a vanishing distance. Faces are now oriented away from a point inside the polytope. Such stacks used to sink, spin or be kicked by contacts as deep as the bodies are wide.
- Fix: contacts of a box with a triangle mesh (`MeshCollisionMode::ORIGINAL_MESH`) came one per triangle, each measured as if the triangle were the half-space behind it. Where a triangle crossed the box, a contact was as deep as the box is wide, along a normal lying in a box face: a mesh box resting on a box was pushed sideways, a box on a mesh step slid off its edge, and meshes landing on a box or on a thin plate fell through it. One contact per triangle also left a box lying inside one large triangle of a floor rocking on a single contact 5 to 16 mm deep, and on floors of slivers or of 1 cm squares the contacts kept were all next to one corner, so most boxes dropped on them fell through. Each triangle is now tested against the box along the triangle's normal, the box's axes and the crosses of their edges, and the box leaves it the shortest way, except that it is held off along the surface's normal where that way would only slide it along a flat mesh surface, and along the triangle's own normal where a curved mesh presses into a box face at an angle. Faces against faces touch at the corners of their overlap, each at its own depth; a box's contacts keep every surface it lies against (the floor and both walls of a mesh bin's corner), spread over each. Boxes set down on mesh floors of slivers, of 1 cm squares or of two triangles, also with zero-area triangles that repeat a corner, now rest where they were set. Over 20000 poses of the G1 pelvis box against the hand mesh of `mesh_primitive_proximity`, 2147 of 2634 contacts were deeper than the bodies overlap, by up to 8.5 cm; none are now, and that case takes 75% longer (99th percentile 25 to 59 µs, worst call 51 to 150 µs). See [Collision Detection and Colliders](../CollisionDetection.md).
- Fix: a robot keeps only the first contact of each pair of its own bodies by default (`ArticulatedSystem::setAllowedNumberOfInternalContactsBetweenTwoBodies()`), and a box's contacts with a mesh came in the order found, so the one kept was arbitrary, often a shallow one at the side of a mesh deep in the box. They now come deepest first. Together with the fix above, the hands of `g1_hand_self_collision` sink into the body's boxes at most 5.4 mm instead of 4.5 cm over 24 phases of its motion; its mean step time is unchanged and its 99th percentile drops from 116 to 72 µs.
- Fix: MJCF `contype` and `conaffinity` were loaded as a RaiSim collision group and mask, which RaiSim tests both ways where MuJoCo needs either, and every geom with `conaffinity="0"` was dropped. Gymnasium's Ant, HalfCheetah and Walker2d had no collision bodies and fell through their floors. Geoms now pair up as in MuJoCo: two touch when the `contype` of either shares a bit with the `conaffinity` of the other, and only a geom with both zero is dropped. Each distinct pair becomes a RaiSim group bit, shared by every MJCF file loaded into the world, with a mask that pairs it accordingly, so geoms of different files also pair up as in MuJoCo; MuJoCo's default pair (1, 1) is RaiSim's default group, and the geoms that touch it also touch RaiSim's ground and heightmaps, which no MJCF geom did. The bundled `walker2d.xml`, edited to `conaffinity="1"` to touch its floor, is back to Gymnasium's `conaffinity="0"`: with 1 its legs touch each other, as they would in MuJoCo. See [World](../WorldSystem.md).
- Fix: when a frame for a viewer did not fit in `RaisimServer`'s send buffer (a heightmap of 2,900 x 2,900 samples is enough), the server dropped the viewer but kept the object, sensor, visual and chart locks it held while writing the frame. The next viewer then deadlocked the server thread while it held the World lock, so `integrateWorldThreadSafe()` never returned. The locks are now released, and `integrateWorldThreadSafe()` also releases the World lock when its callback or the step throws.
- Fix: `ArticulatedSystem::setControlMode()` kept the implicit PD terms of the previous mode in the mass matrix. A robot switched from PD to force control still moved as if damped by its gains. Gains set with `setPGains()` and `setDGains()` in the force mode and then switched on with `setControlMode()` acted without their implicit part, so stiff gains diverged: a 0.1 kg pendulum with gains 20 and 5 at a 1 ms step reached 8e6 rad in 50 steps. The implicit terms were also refreshed in `World::integrate1()` only, so a controller that switched the mode or set gains, damping or springs between `integrate1()` and `integrate2()` ran that step with the implicit terms of the previous settings; `integrate2()` now refreshes them as well.
- Fix: restoring a World checkpoint kept a granular system's lookup of the grains in each cell as it was in the state left behind. The cells were restored, and the next step kept the lookup while the grains occupied the same cells, so a replay missed most grain contacts (351 of 5068 candidate pairs in a cube of 512 grains) or read past the list of cells. The restore now rebuilds the lookup.
- Fix: a heightmap's NaN or infinite height samples, which it accepts, now make holes of the cell triangles around them for every body, granular media and rays. A sphere or an `ORIGINAL_MESH` mesh touching such a triangle got a contact at a NaN position and became NaN within a few hundred steps, a sphere just past the edge of the map, or a cylinder sunk into the terrain, beside such a sample crashed the program, a block of equal infinite samples under a box, capsule or cylinder threw an uncaught exception, a capsule sunk beside such a sample on a large map stopped the program with a non-finite update, a map of such samples only had a NaN bounding box, an infinite sample made the whole map invisible to rays, rays missed the finite triangle sharing a cell with a hole, and a box, capsule, cylinder or convex mesh fell through more or fewer triangles depending on the value and on which corner of a cell held it. Maps without such samples behave exactly as before.
- Fix: RaiSim read and wrote the numbers of its files in the locale the application set. In a locale with a decimal comma (German, French, Russian and many others; a Qt application sets it from the environment), every single number of a world file, URDF or MJCF read only up to its `.` (`radius="0.5"` read 0, `mass="1.5"` read 1), a list such as `xyz="0 0 0.5"` was a fatal error, an OBJ mesh lost its vertices, a text heightmap, an MJCF tendon range and an rscene file failed to load, and the OBJ, MTL and CoACD cache files RaiSim writes, the colors `RaisimServer` sends the viewer and an rscene object's appearance got decimal commas and grouped digits, which the viewer and other readers took for other numbers. Numbers are now read and written with a `.` decimal point and no digit grouping in every locale. See [World Configuration File](../WorldConfigurationFile.md).
- Fix (changes names made from template math): a template math expression (`{...}` in a world or URDF template) was replaced by its result with six decimals, so `{pi/1000}` became 0.003142 and anything below 5e-7 became 0. The result is now the shortest text that reads back as exactly the same number, so a whole number has no decimal point: a name made from math changes (`link_{2*3}` is now `link_6`, was `link_6.000000`), and other values keep all their digits (`{1/3}` is 0.3333333333333333, `{0.1+0.2}` 0.30000000000000004). A result that is not finite (`{1/0}`) is now a fatal error. See [World Configuration File](../WorldConfigurationFile.md).
- Fix: the constant `pi` in a template math expression (`{...}` in a world or URDF template) never advanced the parser, which then added tokens until it ran out of memory. An unmatched `(` or a function name without `(`, as in `{expression}`, read past the end of the expression and could crash. Such expressions, and ones that used to load with a part silently dropped (a trailing operator as in `{1+}`, or a malformed number as in `{1.2.3}`, read as 1.2), are now fatal errors, so a file containing one no longer loads. Numbers in expressions are now read with `.` as the decimal point whatever the C locale; under a comma-decimal locale `{1.5}` was read as 1.
- Fix: on an elastic deformable (`youngsModulus` > 0), a distance constraint between coincident particles, which welds a mesh seam, had the compliance 0 / 0, and the whole object turned NaN as soon as the seam pulled apart. It now gets the compliance 1 / (E thickness) of every other length (or is rigid with `crossSectionArea`). A deformable particle that diverged (a NaN or infinite position or velocity) now makes no contact and leaves the contacts of the rest of its object alone, self contacts included. Its cell in the grids that find self contacts and deformable contacts was the integer conversion of NaN, which wrote far outside the grids and crashed; its contacts with other bodies had NaN positions and full depths; a NaN first particle made the object's bounding box NaN, so the rest of the object fell through the floor; and a particle flung by an infinite force, or by one so large that its squared speed overflows, made the contact margin of the whole object infinite, so every particle touched everything at NaN depths and the object and the bodies lying on it turned NaN. The margin of the other particles now stays finite; a body that a finite but hypersonic particle hits still takes the blow. A rigid body that diverged now stops the simulation with a fatal error whatever the size of the scene and the broadphase; only scenes of 35 or more collision bodies under the default sweep and prune stopped before, while the others gave it contacts at NaN positions, and over a heightmap it read past the height samples. Like every fatal error it calls the fatal callback (`RaiSimMsg::setFatalCallback()`), which exits the process by default, so one diverged world ends a process that steps several. A body that collides with nothing (collision group or mask 0) is not checked, and one parked at an infinite position no longer stretches a heightmap's bounding box or the multi-box pruning grid to infinity (which stopped the simulation, naming the heightmap). The multi-box pruning grid now spans only the bodies that collide, so scenes using it that hold bodies colliding with nothing find their contacts in a different order and do not reproduce 2.10.2 exactly. `AABB::overlaps` now reports no overlap for a NaN bound. A self-colliding cloth with particles past +-1e308 no longer hangs.
- Fix: the acceleration of a spherical joint, which like its velocity is in the child body frame, was added as a world-frame vector to the body accelerations behind `getFrameAcceleration()`, the IMU and the joint forces of the inverse dynamics (`getForceAtJointInWorldFrame()`, `getTorqueAtJointInWorldFrame()`). Away from the identity orientation the accelerations were off, and a free ball joint reported a transmitted torque.
- Fix: the PD controller and the springs of a spherical joint took the rotation from the joint to its target the long way around whenever the quaternion of that rotation had a negative w, as for a target given as the negative of a quaternion (the same orientation) or a joint that had turned past 180 degrees: a target 10 degrees away was pushed toward 350 degrees the other way, with 35 times the torque.
- Fix: `SingleBodyObject::getVelocity(localIdx, pos_b, vel_w)`, the velocity of a body point given relative to the body frame origin, added the spin about the origin to the velocity of the center of mass instead of the spin about the center of mass. Bodies with an offset center of mass (meshes, compounds, `setCom()`) reported wrong point velocities, and tendons attached to them, grains touching them and the viewer's interaction wire used them. For such a body `getVelocity(0, {0, 0, 0}, v)` is now the velocity of the body frame origin, while `getVelocity(0, v)` stays the velocity of the center of mass.
- Fix: `ArticulatedSystem` setters given a vector of the wrong size (`setGeneralizedCoordinate()`, `setGeneralizedVelocity()` and `setGeneralizedForce()` with an Eigen or `VecDyn` vector, `setState()`, `setJointDamping()`, `setActuationLimits()`, `setRotorInertia()`) stored it, and the dynamics then read and wrote past it: 12 torques for an 18-DOF robot overran the forward dynamics' buffers, and `setState()` with short vectors corrupted the heap. A body index past the last body read or wrote past the body arrays in `setExternalForce()`, `setExternalTorque()`, `setExternalTorqueInBodyFrame()`, `setConstraintForce()`, `setMass()` (which corrupted the heap), `addSpring()`, `getDenseJacobian()` and `getDenseRotationalJacobian()`, and in release builds the dense Jacobians were written past a matrix smaller than 3 x DOF. Each is now a fatal error naming the sizes (a dense Jacobian of any size other than 3 x DOF, as debug builds already required); the initializer-list overloads and the PD setters were already fatal.
- Fix: a cylinder against a triangle mesh (`MeshCollisionMode::ORIGINAL_MESH`) was tested as the capsule of its axis and radius, with one contact per triangle on the capsule's surface. Standing on a mesh floor it rested on a single contact under its axis, a radius minus a half height too high (a cylinder of radius 0.3 m and height 0.4 m stood 0.1 m above a mesh plate), and toppled at a touch; tilted, its depth was that of the capsule's rounded end; near a mesh it got contacts where the capsule reached past its caps (26 of 512 poses of the torso cylinder of `mesh_primitive_proximity`, separated from the hand mesh, had some). Each triangle is now tested against the cylinder itself, and a cylinder touches a mesh as a box does: standing on a face around its rim, lying on it along the lowest line of its side, tipped onto its rim at the rim's lowest point, each contact at its own depth, held off a flat mesh surface along its normal. Cylinders now stand, lie, roll and tip off edges on mesh floors of any triangulation as on a box, and a pair allowed fewer than eight contacts (`contact::ContactSettings::maxContactsPerPair`) keeps its deepest one. `mesh_primitive_proximity` takes 56% less time for the torso cylinder and 12% more for the limb cylinder, whose 99th percentile goes from 31 to 62 µs; in `g1_hand_self_collision` the hands move differently, touching on 3327 steps instead of 3312 and sinking about as deep (at most 17 instead of 16 mm), and a step takes 14.4 instead of 12.1 µs (99th percentile 99 instead of 73 µs).
- Fix: `ArticulatedSystem::clearExternalForcesAndTorques()` kept the bodies of the cleared forces and torques, and the inverse dynamics attached the ones set afterwards to those bodies, so the joint forces and torques it reports (`getForceAtJointInWorldFrame()`, `getTorqueAtJointInWorldFrame()`) stayed wrong until a step ended with an external force set, and repeated clears piled up stale entries; the motion itself was right.
- Fix: the first update of a `SpinningLidar` after the sensor was created or loaded swept all the turns made since world time 0: a 10 Hz LiDAR first updated at 30 s, on a robot added to a running world or in a world loaded from a file saved then, cast 300 turns of rays in that update. It now sweeps at most one update interval. A spinning LiDAR of a URDF or MJCF robot also ignored the robot's root body instead of the body it is mounted on, so a LiDAR on any other body, inside the collision body of its link (its housing), hit the housing with every ray. It now ignores the body it is mounted on, as a depth camera does. See [Sensors](../Sensors.md).
- Fix: the `anchor` of an MJCF `<equality><connect>` was taken in the frame RaiSim gives `body1`, the frame of the body's joint (of its last joint if it has several), instead of `body1`'s own frame, so the pin was off by the joint's `pos`: a connect at a hinge's pivot, which adds nothing in MuJoCo, held the hinge still. The anchor is now in `body1`'s frame, as in MuJoCo. See [World](../WorldSystem.md).
- Fix: with swept CCD (`contact::ContactSettings::sweptCcdEnabled`), a body approaching the ground or a heightmap more slowly than the speed that closes its gap in one step, as one braked by a force, got an impulse anyway: with no sliding velocity this divided by zero, and the body turned NaN in a step, and a body that can only move along the contact normal, such as a slider, was pulled toward the surface to that speed. Such a body now moves on untouched until the step that would carry it past the surface, which holds it to the speed that reaches the surface.
- Fix: `World::removeObject()` left the last step's contacts in place until the next step: the contacts of the objects that touched the removed one pointed at its freed collision bodies (reading the pair's collision body read freed memory), and the contacts naming the removed object or the object moved into its world index by world index named the wrong objects. The last step's contacts (`Object::getContacts()` and `World::getContactProblem()`) are now cleared; the next step finds the remaining ones. Removing an object between `World::integrate1()` and `integrate2()`, which works on the contacts and constraint rows `integrate1()` made, now throws `std::logic_error`, as adding or removing a tendon or a wire there already did.
- Fix: a revolute joint's axis changed through `ArticulatedSystem::getJointAxis_P()`, as model randomization does, was ignored when the joint's axis was a coordinate axis (`1 0 0`, `0 -1 0` and the like) where the model was loaded, as for most robots: a hinge turned from the y axis to the x axis kept turning about y. The new axis is now used from the next kinematics update on (`setGeneralizedCoordinate()`, `updateKinematics()`, or the end of the next step), also after a checkpoint captured before that update is restored. Such a checkpoint also restored a closed-loop system's constraint analysis as if no joint placement had changed, so a constraint direction the change made necessary went missing (a tilted parallelogram's closure drifted 1 cm apart); it now restores the pending change. See [Modifying the Model in Code](../articulated_system/ModifyingTheModel.md).
- Fix: after `World::setWorldTime()` moved the time back, as at the start of a new episode, RaiSim-updated spinning LiDARs and depth cameras, and the cameras a `RaisimServer` viewer renders, stopped updating until the world time passed their last update again, and a LiDAR its owner updated returned empty scans; the IMU already started over. They now update at their next update (the next step for RaiSim-updated sensors), its scan sweeping the time since world time 0 (at most one update interval), and go on at their rates; the new `Sensor::isUpdateDue()` tells when an update is due. See [Sensors](../Sensors.md).
- Fix: an articulated system with a joint that moves nothing -- in a URDF, a link without `<inertial>` on a movable joint, with no mass below it -- turned its whole state into NaN in its first step. Stepping it is now a fatal error naming the joint, as it is for a ball joint or a floating base whose inertia vanishes along some direction (a ball joint carrying a thin rod, a massless base whose only mass hangs from a hinge). A state that has turned NaN for other reasons steps on as before. Such a model still loads, for its kinematics, and a joint whose rotor inertia, damping or PD gains give it an inertia still steps. A URDF mass that is negative or not finite, or an inertia that is not finite, now fails to load. See [Creating an Articulated System](../articulated_system/Creation.md).
- Fix: a URDF whose root link is `world` welded the link below it to the world whatever their joint was: a cart on a `prismatic` rail from the world lost its rail (a cart-pole had one degree of freedom instead of two), and a `floating` joint made a fixed base. A `floating` joint now makes a floating base, starting at the joint's origin, and any other joint that moves keeps `world` as the fixed, massless base from which the link moves on its joint (an `<inertial>` on the `world` link is ignored); a `fixed` joint is unchanged. See [Creating an Articulated System](../articulated_system/Creation.md).
- Fix: an object added between `World::integrate1()` and `integrate2()` had none of the state `integrate1()` makes for every object, and with sleeping enabled `integrate2()` read its sleep state past the end of the list. Adding an object there now throws `std::logic_error`, as removing one does, and leaves the world as it was. See [World](../WorldSystem.md).
- Fix: `World::addGranularBox()` checked its options in debug builds only. In a release build a spacing of zero, a negative one, or one too small to move a coordinate of the box (1e-12 m at 1e6 m) never advanced a row and made particles until memory ran out, unless `maxParticles` stopped it. Every build now rejects these, a radius that is not positive and finite, corners that are not finite or out of order, and options that make no particle.
- Fix: a granular particle outside a periodic boundary was moved back one width at a time, so a particle far out (one thrown at 1e300 m/s, say) kept the step from ever ending: past 2^53 widths, a width is below the coordinate's resolution and subtracting it changes nothing. It now goes back all the widths at once; one width out, the result is the same as before to the last bit, and a coordinate that is not finite is left as it is. The `wrappedParticles` statistic still counts the widths. `GranularSystem::setPeriodicBoundary()` now rejects corners that are not finite.
- Fix: `ArticulatedSystem::massMatrixVecMul()` was declared `inline` in the header but defined only in the library, so a call to it did not link. It is an ordinary member function now, which checks the size of its input and resizes its output.
- Fix: malformed world files crashed instead of reporting what was wrong. A compound child with more than four `object_param` values wrote past its parameters, a compound object class that contains itself (directly or through other classes) was expanded from the list it was growing, reading freed memory without end, and a material file without `<material>` read past an empty list. Each is a fatal error with a message now. A compound child also needs exactly the values its shape takes (box 3, cylinder and capsule 2, sphere 1), finite and positive (a capsule's height may be zero).
- Fix: contact settings were used unchecked. A broadphase grid of zero cells per axis crashed the first step, and an unknown broadphase type or a NaN cell size silently found no contacts at all. Settings RaiSim cannot use are fatal errors now, from `World::setContactSettings()`, a world file's `<simulation>` or a scene alike, and the world keeps its settings. A multi-box pruning grid of more than 2^24 cells (16.8 million; the default is 2^21), which ran out of memory or past the range of its cell indices, now gets larger cells, so a scene that asked for one finds its contacts in a different order. See [World](../WorldSystem.md).
- Fix: a STATIC single body kept the external forces and torques set on it, since no step consumed them, and they all acted at once when it turned DYNAMIC: 1000 steps of 1 N moved a 1 kg box at 10 m/s in one step. The list behind `getExternalForce()` grew by one entry each step. A STATIC body ignores external forces and torques now, and a body that turns STATIC (a compound too) drops the ones still pending. An articulated system took external forces without waking up: asleep, it kept them all, and they acted at once when something woke it (2000 steps of a 0.5 N lift on a sleeping 1 kg robot gave it 0.99 m/s in one step). A force or torque wakes it now, as it does a single body.
- Fix: `ArticulatedSystem::getCompositeCOM()[0]` and `getCompositeInertia()[0]`, the whole system's after `getMassMatrix()`, were wrong: for a fixed-base system the COM kept its first value and the inertia was the root link's own, and for a floating base the inertia was taken about the base joint, not about the composite COM as for every other body.
- Fix: a URDF spherical joint with a `stiffness` and no `spring_mount` got the rest orientation (0, 0, 0, 0), which is no rotation, so its spring did nothing. The rest orientation is the identity now. A spherical joint spring given a zero rest orientation in code (`Child::joint.springMount` or `ArticulatedSystem::addSpring()`) is a fatal error, and a non-unit one is normalized. See [Creating an Articulated System](../articulated_system/Creation.md).
- Fix: `RaisimServer` accepted a `CR_SET_GC` request whose floating-base or ball-joint quaternion was zero (or not of unit length): the robot's whole state turned NaN, which the next contact detection reported as a fatal error. Such a request is rejected now, as `CR_SET_POSE`'s quaternion already was.
- Fix: when the viewer disconnected while dragging an object, the drag wire stayed, and `integrateWorldThreadSafe()` kept pulling the object toward where the cursor last was. The wire is released when the client goes.
- Fix: `RaisimServer::processRequests()` failing part way through a client's message left the connection open, so a program serving the client from its own loop (`acceptConnection()` and `processRequests()`, as `synchronous_server_update.cpp` does) read the rest of the message as new messages and never replied; `acceptConnection()` then replaced the client without closing it. A failed `processRequests()` closes the connection now, and `acceptConnection()` closes a client still connected. With no client connected, `waitForMessageFromClient()` returns `true` at once and `processRequests()` returns `false`, so such a loop goes on to accept the next client, as it did when the closed client's connection ended.
- Fix: a frame larger than the socket's buffers went to the viewer in one blocking `send()`, so a viewer that stopped reading held the server thread, and `killServer()`, for as long as it stayed connected. A frame now leaves in pieces as the viewer makes room, within 10 s plus a second per 256 KiB (as a viewer's message must arrive), and `killServer()` ends the wait within 0.1 s. See [Raisim Server](../RaisimServer.md).
- Fix: an open triangle mesh (`MeshCollisionMode::ORIGINAL_MESH`), such as a plate, dropped on a heightmap sank into it on one side, flipped over and kept spinning (16 rad/s after 3 s). The heightmap is solid below its surface, but only the mesh triangles crossing the surface got contacts, each at a point of the crossing and as deep as the triangle's lowest point. - Fix: two bodies of an articulated system touching each other kept the first contacts the narrowphase reported, up to `ArticulatedSystem::getAllowedNumberOfInternalContactsBetweenTwoBodies()` (1 by default), instead of the deepest: a pair touching in two places could keep a 2 cm overlap and leave a 5 cm one unresolved. The deepest are kept now.
- Fix: with sleeping on (the default), a fixed-base articulated system's base became a moving body for collision: the system's own type, DYNAMIC, was written into every one of its bodies, so a base set into the ground got contacts with it at every step, also after sleeping was turned off. Its base stays STATIC now.
- Fix: rolling and spinning friction against a kinematic body acted on a body's own turn instead of its turn relative to the kinematic one: a ball at the middle of a kinematic turntable was held still instead of turning with it. They act on the relative turn now, as sliding friction does on the relative velocity.
- Fix: a contact whose normal impulse dropped to zero during the contact solve kept the rolling and spinning friction impulses that the normal impulse had allowed, a torque at a contact that no longer pressed. They go with the normal impulse now.
- Fix: swept CCD (`contact::ContactSettings::sweptCcdEnabled`) took the speed that closes a speculative contact's gap off the bounce, too: a ball 9 cm above the ground at 10 m/s with a restitution of 1 turned at 0.9 m/s in the air, and at 0.5 it was slowed in the air and bounced at 1.2 m/s on the next step. A body that reaches the surface within the step now bounces off it with the surface's restitution, as one touching it would; the bounce starts up to the gap early. The gap's speed was also carried through the error reduction parameter, so with `World::setERP(0)` a speculative contact stopped the body at its gap; it reaches the surface now. - Fix: a cylinder lying along a mesh edge parallel to its axis (`MeshCollisionMode::ORIGINAL_MESH`), such as a pipe lying in a slot on its two edges, touched each edge at one point only: it tipped about its two contacts, sank 6 mm between the edges as it settled and was still rocking at 0.2 rad/s after 3 s. It now reads and checks the whole file first and changes nothing unless it all loads. - Fix: a granular particle's contact history with a body (the stretch of its tangential spring) was kept under the body's world index, which removing another object gives to the last object: the history of a body that moved to a new index was dropped, and went over to whichever object then had its old one. Capsules, spheres and convex meshes had such cutoffs too, below 1e-6 and 1e-8, which mattered only for overlaps under about 1e-6 m. A support point now depends on the direction alone.
- Fix: two cylinders with nearly parallel axes touching cap on cap (within 2.5 degrees) got contacts up to 3% of the radius off both surfaces and up to 0.4 mm deeper than they overlapped: the depth was measured from each rim in its cylinder's middle plane, not from the rim of the cap, and the overlap of the caps was taken around the bodies' centers, which a tilt puts beside the caps' centers. Tilted caps touching rim on rim went to the general convex test, whose normal was far from the axes, and equal cylinders one over the other, offset by up to about 1e-12 m, got no contact at all. Each contact is now a point of a cap's rim inside the other cylinder, midway between the two caps: the deepest one (a rim's deepest point or a corner where the rims cross), and others spread over the overlap. - New: `World::getLifetimeIdentity()` identifies a World for its whole lifetime, even when a later World reuses its address, and `World::getSharedRscene()` shares its immutable `.rscene` scene.
- Visual-only heightmap edits (`updateVisualHeight()`, `updateVisualHeightPatch()`) and deformable integration advance `Object::getRenderRevision()`.
- New: `HeightMap::getHeightRowRevisions()`, one revision per sample row that advances whenever a height in the row changes (by any update method, a checkpoint restore, or a deformable terrain sinking). Unlike `isUpdated()` and the visual dirty region, the revisions are never cleared, so any number of renderers can follow them. Whole-map updates advance only the rows whose heights differ.
- `updateVisualHeightPatch()` that leaves every sample at its value (bit for bit) reports no update: `isUpdated()`, the visual dirty region and the render revision stay as they were.
- Fix: `ArticulatedSystem::solveIK()` that applied a solution moved the links without advancing `getRenderRevision()`, so renderers that cache by that revision could keep drawing the old pose.
- Fix: a URDF `<sphere>`, `<cylinder>` or `<capsule>` left the shape parameters it does not set (`VisObject::visShapeParam`, `CollisionDefinition::shapeParam`) uninitialized; they are now zero.
